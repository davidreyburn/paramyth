TILE MAPPER — assign sheet cells to tile types

Pick a type, pick a sheet, then click cells. variants and overlay types toggle a cell in or out of their variant list. nineslice types (walls) take a single click that sets the top-left of their 3×3 block — a wall's tile is chosen by which neighbours share its type, which is why it cannot be a random variant. Assigned cells carry the type's colour; cells drawn dim hold art nothing uses yet. The preview is a real generated room in the current mapping, fully lit so the art is judged rather than the lighting.
