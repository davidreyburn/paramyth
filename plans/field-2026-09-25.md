# The Field, and three stats renamed

*2026-09-25. DJ: "change Finesse to Swift, Keen to Insight, Bearing to Charm,
then update the docs, then add the field. When you're done with the field, move
the stairs out of the company camp — they wouldn't sleep next to that. You may
need to generate a tile for the outdoor sections."*

## 1. The rename

Finesse → **Swift**, Keen → **Insight**, Bearing → **Charm**. Might, Vigor and
Lore stay. Code: the stat record, the provenance table's `read` column (every
mark the eye finds is now read by Insight), the status page, the tests. Docs:
the stat table and every sentence that names one. Saves: `restore` renames the
keys of an old delta so a run in progress keeps its numbers.

## 2. The Field

`design/world-shape.md`: *"Surface Barrowlands. Open, traversable, dotted with
mausoleum mouths, salvage camps, bandit holds, broken monuments. Dangerous but
survivable. The hub."*

**Shape.** Floor −1 stops being one authored room and becomes a floor like any
other: a 3×2 grid of rooms, all linked, all present. Room 0 is the Company Camp,
authored as before. The other five are generated open ground. One of them — at
least two rooms from the camp — holds the mausoleum mouth. `floorPlan(seed,
site, −1)` returns this; `roomTiles` routes room 0 to the camp and the rest to
`fieldRoom`.

**Open ground, generated.** A new tile type, `GRASS`: walkable, not solid, drawn
from three generated 20×20 cells in the project sheet (a dark ground with hashed
light tufts — data once generated, like DJ's icons) in a fixed green, so the
outdoors is green at every site and does not take the stratum's stone tone.
Each field room is grass with hashed boulder clusters (`RUBBLE` — obstruction
is content, and the cap breaks it) and one or two broken monuments (`WALL`
stubs, which the nine-slice already draws as masonry). Room edges are **open the
whole length** where a link exists and a boulder line where the world ends, so
crossing between rooms is walking, not doorways. The camp's east and south
sides open onto the Field the same way; its north and west palisade stays.

**The mouth.** A small mausoleum front stamped in its room: a walled 4×4 with
the stairs inside and the approach open to the south, as the camp had. Floor
0's up-stair returns you to that room and that tile, as it always did.

**Not friendly.** `friendly()` was "the whole surface". It becomes "room 0 of
the surface": steel comes out on the Field, caps can be set there, and a dog
sometimes hunts there (one field room in four; never the camp). `surface()` —
daylit, no lantern — stays the whole floor.

**Stations** draw and answer only in the camp room; they were keyed to the
surface and would have appeared in every field room.

## 3. The stairs leave the camp

Follows from 2: the camp map loses its `>>` block and gains two open sides. Its
reach flood starts from the opening rather than the mouth. The two gates that
said "the camp has a mouth down" are rewritten to say the Field has one and the
camp does not, and that the way down and back is still a round trip.

## Gates

- the three stats exist under their new names and none under the old
- an old save's stats survive a load under the new names
- every mark's `read` names a stat that exists
- floor −1 has six rooms; the camp is room 0; the mouth is at least two rooms away
- the camp has no stair; exactly one field room has one, on floor, reachable
- the camp opens onto the Field and the Field is walkable camp → mouth by
  crossing open edges
- the round trip: stand on the mouth, descend, land on the way up, ascend, land
  on the mouth
- grass is a tile type with art, walkable, and never appears underground
- the camp is friendly; the rest of the Field is not; nothing hunts in the camp
- stations answer only in the camp room
- the surface stays daylit in every field room

## Not in scope

Contents on the Field (salvage camps, caches), bandits, weather, a clock, and
walking between sites across the surface. The shape leaves room for all of them:
a field room is a room, and a site's Field could link to the next site's at its
edge when that is wanted.
