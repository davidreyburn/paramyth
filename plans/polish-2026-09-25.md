# Polish, 2026-09-25: the fade, the pop, and the bomb's colour

*DJ: "a quick dithered fade when you change floors"; "when an enemy dies, a
subtle effect animation, such as a thin bubble of red (or another colour
depending on enemy) popping"; "the bomb should be a consistent colour, not
per-floor tint."*

## The constraint that shapes all three

`rendering is deterministic for a fixed state` is a gate. Two draws of one
state must be identical, so nothing on the glass may be keyed to the wall
clock. An effect is therefore a **tick stamped in the delta** plus a draw that
is a pure function of `s.tick - stamp`. Both effects are hashed and replayed,
like `say` is, and both prune themselves in `step()` as physics does.

## 1. The dithered fade

- **Delta:** `arrivedAt` — the tick of the last floor change. Set in
  `enterFloor` and in `die()` (waking in camp is a floor change). A new state
  starts at `-FADE_TICKS`, so the game does not fade in at birth; only a
  change of floor does. `FADE_TICKS = 18` (three tenths of a second).
- **Draw:** an ordered dither, 4×4 Bayer, over the world and the light and
  under the HUD. Coverage level 16 → 0 across the fade. Seventeen 4×4 pattern
  tiles are built once and applied with `createPattern`, so a full-screen
  dither is one `fillRect`, not 230k pixel writes.
- **Gates:** on arrival the view is all void; halfway it is a dither (a
  quarter to three quarters void, and not a solid block); after `FADE_TICKS`
  the view is untouched. Headless: `enterFloor` and `die` stamp `arrivedAt`.

## 2. The pop

- **Delta:** `pops: [{ x, y, at, kind }]`, pushed by `hurtFoe` when the blow
  kills; pruned after `POP_TICKS = 10`.
- **Draw:** a one-pixel ring by the midpoint circle algorithm — crisp pixels,
  no anti-aliased arc — radius `2 + 2·age`. Whole for the first half of its
  life, then every other pixel: the bubble thins and breaks. Colour from the
  pack per kind (`foes[kind].pop`): rot-red for the dog, bone for the
  Sentinel, `P.rot` when unspecified.
- **Gates:** a kill pushes a pop of the right kind; it expires; replay holds
  with pops in flight. On the glass: a fresh pop draws, an expired one does
  not, and it draws the same twice.

## 3. The bomb's colour

- The pack already has the mechanism: `items.<kind>.tone` is a fixed two-tone
  pair that wins over the stratum's ("wood is wood at every depth"). The cap
  gets iron: `{ light: '#8e8983', dark: '#2b2521' }`.
- **Gate:** the cap has a fixed tone, and it is not any stratum's.

## Not in scope

A fade *out* before the stairs would mean delaying the transition by
`FADE_TICKS`, which is a simulation change for a presentation effect. The
fade-in alone reads as the change of floor; if it does not, that is the next
thing to try.
